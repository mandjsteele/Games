How to play
  • 11 rounds. Round 1 deals 3 cards, round 11 deals 13.
  • The rank matching the number of cards dealt is wild (3s in round 1 … Kings in round 11). Jokers are always wild.
  • On your turn draw from the deck or discard pile, then discard one card.
  • Go out when every remaining card is in a book (3+ of the same rank) or a run (3+ in a row, same suit).
  • After someone goes out, everyone else gets one last turn. Cards you can't meld count against you: face value, J=11, Q=12, K=13, wilds 20, Jokers 50.
  • Lowest total after 11 rounds wins.
